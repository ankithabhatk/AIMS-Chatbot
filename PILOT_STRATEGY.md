# Pilot Strategy (Converting Trials to Revenue)

**Goal:** The goal is NOT to "test the product". The goal is to prove revenue impact.
**Positioning:** DO NOT call it a "free trial".
> *"Let’s run this with your real student inquiries and measure the impact."*

---

## 1. Pilot Structure
- **Duration:** 7–14 days max.
- **Scope:** Website widget or WhatsApp integration. Run on real student queries.
- **What to track (ONLY 4 metrics):**
  1. Conversations started
  2. Avg messages per student
  3. Decision signals
  4. Application intent ("how to apply")

---

## 2. Step-by-Step Execution

### Step 1: Set Expectations (Critical)
> *"The goal of this pilot is to see if we can move students closer to applying — not just answering questions."*

### Step 2: Define the Success Metric
> *"How many inquiries do you usually get per week? If we can convert even a small percentage of these into stronger application intent, that’s a win."*

### Step 3: Quick Setup (Speed Matters)
> *"We can set this up in 1–2 days."*
*(Speed builds trust. Use a simple web snippet or WhatsApp integration).*

### Step 4: Run Live (Your Secret Advantage)
During the pilot, monitor the backend manually daily. Look for:
- Confusion loops
- Drop-offs
- Decision signals

### Step 5: Mid-Pilot Check (Day 3-5)
> *"We’re seeing students engage and move toward decisions — early signals look good."*
*(Keep them warm).*

### Step 6: Final Review Call (Most Important)
Start with: *"Let me show you what happened."*

**Present the Data:**
1. **Engagement:** *"X students interacted, average conversation depth was Y messages."*
2. **Decision Signals:** *"Z students reached a clear course decision."*
3. **Application Intent:** *"N students asked about applying."*

Then anchor the reality:
> *"This is where your conversions start improving."*

---

## 3. The Close

> *"The system is already working with your students. We can keep it running and expand it further."*
> 
> *"Would you like to continue this as part of your admissions process?"*

### Pricing Transition (If they hesitate)
> *"We can start with a simple monthly setup and refine it as we go."*

### Power Close
> *"You’ve already seen how students respond — stopping now means losing that momentum."*

---

## Core Philosophy
The pilot is NOT about asking *"Does the system work?"*
It is about proving *"Did the college SEE value?"*

**The Final Line:**
> *"We’ve already proven this with your students — now it’s about scaling it."*
