# Objection Handling Script (Live Call)

**Core Rule:** When they object, do NOT defend or explain immediately.
1. **Acknowledge**
2. **Reframe**
3. **Answer**

---

### Objection 1: “We already have counselors”
**Acknowledge:** *"That’s actually perfect."* (Pause)
**Reframe & Answer:** *"This doesn’t replace your counselors — it prepares students before they reach them. So instead of answering basic questions, your team gets students who are already clear and ready."*
**Anchor:** *"It basically removes the repetitive load and lets your counselors focus on serious applicants."*

### Objection 2: “We already have a chatbot”
**Acknowledge:** *"Most colleges do."* (Pause)
**Reframe & Answer:** *"But those systems answer questions. This one helps students decide."*
**Anchor:** *"The drop-off doesn’t happen because students don’t get answers — it happens because they can’t choose."*

### Objection 3: “Will this actually increase admissions?”
**Acknowledge:** *"That’s the only thing we focus on."*
**Reframe & Answer:** *"We track when a student moves from confusion → clarity → decision → application. So instead of guessing, you can actually see where conversions improve."*
**Anchor:** *"Even a small improvement here directly impacts admissions."*

### Objection 4: “Students may not trust AI”
**Acknowledge:** *"That’s a valid concern."*
**Reframe & Answer:** *"That’s why the system is designed to behave like a counselor — not a bot. It guides step-by-step instead of dumping information."*
**Anchor:** *"And your team is always there if needed — this just handles the first layer."*

### Objection 5: “Too expensive”
**Acknowledge:** *"Totally fair."*
**Reframe & Answer:** *"If this helps you convert even 10 additional students..."* (Pause) *"...it more than covers the cost."*
**Anchor:** *"So the real question is not the price — it’s whether it improves conversion."*

### Objection 6: “We need time to think” *(Stall)*
**Acknowledge:** *"Of course — makes sense."*
**Reframe & Answer:** *"Just so I understand — is it more about fit, or timing?"*
**(Diagnose the REAL issue)**

### Objection 7: “Send details, we’ll check later” *(Dangerous)*
**Acknowledge:** *"I can definitely send details."*
**Reframe & Answer:** *"But usually this works best when we try it with real student queries."*
**Anchor:** *"Would you be open to a quick setup and test with your students?"*

### Objection 8: “We’ll get back to you”
**Acknowledge:** *"Sure."*
**Anchor:** *"When would be a good time to follow up — tomorrow or later this week?"* (Lock the time)

---

## Power Moves & Tone
- **Power Move:** *"What matters here is not the technology. It’s whether students are reaching decisions faster."*
- **Silent Close:** After answering an objection, STOP TALKING. Let them think.
- **Tone:** Calm, confident, not desperate.
- **Avoid:** *"Please try"*, *"We are new"*, *"This is AI-powered"*.

## Final Close Line (Always Use)
> *"If you’re open to it, we can set this up and test it with real students — that’s the best way to see the impact."*
