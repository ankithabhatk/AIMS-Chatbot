# Live Demo Script (10-Minute Word-for-Word Flow)

**Total Time:** 10–12 Minutes
**Core Rule:** Do NOT show UI. Show the conversation. Let them read. Do not over-explain the tech.

---

## 0. Opening (30 seconds)
*"Thanks for taking the time. I’ll keep this very simple.*
*I’m going to show you how a confused student turns into an applicant using our system."*

**(Pause)**

*"This is not a chatbot demo — this is an admissions conversion flow."*

## 1. Set Context (1 min)
*"Today, most students don’t drop off because of lack of information. They drop off because they can’t decide. So instead of answering questions, we built a system that guides decisions."*

## 2. Show the Problem (1 min)
*"Let’s take a real scenario. A student comes and says:"*

**(Type or show):**
> "I got 60%, what should I do?"

*"Most systems either give generic answers or dump course lists. Here’s what happens instead."*

## 3. Show Guidance (2 min)
**(Show the system response)**

*"Notice this — it doesn’t just give options.*
*It filters based on marks, suggests relevant courses, and explains why.*
*This reduces confusion immediately."*

## 4. Show Multi-Intent (2 min)
*"Now students don’t ask one question. They think like this:"*

**(Type):**
> "BBA vs BCom which is better?"

*"The system compares both clearly and then pushes toward a decision.*
*This is where most students usually get stuck — and drop off."*

## 5. Show Decision Moment [CRITICAL] (2 min)
*"Let’s say the student says:"*

**(Type):**
> "I think BBA is good"

**(Pause and let them SEE the response)**

*"This is the key moment.*
*Instead of stopping here, the system detects a decision signal and moves the student forward."*

## 6. Show Conversion Flow [CRITICAL] (2 min)
*"Now watch this transition.*
*It validates the choice, shows career direction, and then softly introduces admission.*
*We don’t push links immediately. We wait for intent."*

**(Type):**
> "How to apply?"

**(Show the final response + Call to Action)**

## 7. Drop the Core Value (1 min)
*(Say this slowly)*

*"This is the difference.*
*Most systems answer questions.*
*This system moves students from confusion → decision → application."*

## 8. Business Hook (1 min)
*"Practically, this means:*
- *Your counselors spend less time on basic queries.*
- *Students come pre-decided.*
- *You get higher application intent.*

*In most cases, this replaces 60–70% of repetitive counseling work."*

## 9. Close (Don't Over-talk)
*"We’re onboarding a few colleges right now to refine this with real student data.*
*We can set this up for you in a few days.*

*Would you like to try this with your students and see how it performs?"*

---

## Delivery Rules
**DO:**
- Pause after each stage.
- Let them read responses.
- Keep tone calm.

**DON'T:**
- Explain too much tech.
- Rush.
- Say "AI model / LLM / RAG".

## Power Moves (Use When Needed)
- **If they lean in:** *"This works because we guide decisions — not just answer queries."*
- **If they look confused:** *"Think of it as a digital counselor that never gets tired."*
- **If they ask 'what’s different?':** *"We separate thinking from speaking — logic is controlled, AI only handles communication."*

## The Final Line (Most Important)
*(Before ending the call, anchor them with this)*

> **"If this helps you convert even 10 more students, it pays for itself."**
