# Go-To-Market & Pricing Strategy

## Pricing Strategy (Sell Conversion, Not Usage)

### Stage 1 (First 3-5 Colleges)
**Goal:** Close fast, not maximize revenue. Fast validation.
- **Offer:** ₹15K – ₹30K / month per college
- **Includes:**
  - Full AI Counselor
  - Unlimited usage (don't restrict early)
  - Basic analytics dashboard
  - WhatsApp / Web integration
- **Why this works:** Low friction, easy "yes", fast validation.

### Stage 2 (After Proof)
**Goal:** Transition from a tool to a revenue partner.
- **Hybrid Pricing:** Base + Performance
- **Base:** ₹20K
- **Bonus:** + ₹100–₹300 per qualified lead **OR** + ₹500–₹1500 per application.

### Stage 3 (Scale)
**Goal:** Premium positioning via Tiered Plans.
- **Starter:** ₹25K (Small colleges)
- **Growth:** ₹50K (Mid colleges)
- **Pro:** ₹1L+ (Large institutions)
- **Includes:** Deeper analytics, CRM integration, WhatsApp automation, custom flows.

---

## GTM Strategy (How to Sell)

### Step 1: Find Target Colleges
- Tier 2 / Tier 3 colleges
- Private institutions
- MBA / BBA / BCA focused
- **Why:** They struggle with admissions, are more open, and have faster decision-making.

### Step 2: The Entry Point
**DO NOT pitch the product.** Say this instead:
> *"We built a system that helps students decide faster and increases admission conversions."*

### Step 3: Demo Strategy (Crucial)
**DO NOT show UI. Show the conversation.**
- Student comes in confused -> Guidance -> Comparison -> Decision -> Conversion flow.
- Then drop the anchor:
> *"This replaces 60–70% of counselor workload."*

### Step 4: Close Fast
> *"We’re onboarding a few colleges right now to refine the system. We can set this up for you in a few days."*

---

## Objection Handling

**"We already have counselors"**
> *"This doesn’t replace them — it filters and prepares students before they talk to your team."*

**"We already have a chatbot"**
> *"Most chatbots answer questions. This system helps students decide — that’s where conversions happen."*

**"Will this actually increase admissions?"**
> *"We track decision signals and application intent — so you’ll see exactly where conversions improve."*

## 30-Day Execution Plan
- **Week 1:** Finalize demo, list 20 target colleges, begin outreach.
- **Week 2:** Run 10-15 demos, close 1-2 deals.
- **Week 3:** Deploy, collect data.
- **Week 4:** Refine pitch, close 2-3 more deals.

---

## The Closing Line
> *"If this helps you convert even 10 more students, it pays for itself."*
